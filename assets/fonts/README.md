Fonts, all under the SIL Open Font License (the OFL-*.txt files): Didact Gothic (the display face, `--display`), Yellowtail (the script face, `--script`), Monoton (capitals in parallel lines like ROLLING on the title sign, `--lines`), and seven more for wherever a face is chosen: Tilt Neon (`--neon`, one bent tube), Limelight (`--marquee`, a theatre front), Poiret One (`--deco`, thin and geometric), Sacramento (`--hand`, a fine joined script), Bungee Inline (`--inline`, sign capitals with a line down each stroke), Rye (`--saloon`, a spurred poster face) and VT323 (`--board`, the dots of a tote board or a till). A Roller's sign can set its name and its class in any of them (Build mode › Neon, `nameFont` and `clsFont`), and so can a card's print (Build mode › Cards). To change a typeface, replace its `.woff2` here and update `css/fonts.css` and `FONTS` in `js/content/assets.js`.
